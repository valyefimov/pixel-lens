The code, text, icons and illustrations in this repository were written for Pixel Lens. No code, text, icons or images were copied from other browser extensions or their websites. Do not paste code, copy, screenshots, icons or other assets from other products into this repository, and do not decompile or copy from other extensions. ## Using Pixel Lens on other sites

Pixel Lens reads and temporarily changes pages inside your own browser. Edits are only visible to you and are not sent to the site. The asset tools download files that the page already sent to your browser; that does not give you a license to them. Respect the copyright of the sites you inspect and their terms of use.

## Privacy

See the [privacy policy](https://pixel-lens.yefimov.tech/privacy). In short: no analytics, no accounts, and nothing leaves your machine unless you turn on the agent bridge, which only talks to `127.0.0.1`.
